“RAFA by Farheen Ravindra was born with a vision to create a new generation of Hair & Skin Care — where the timeless wisdom of nature meets the possibilities of the future.”

For decades, traditional hair colouring was divided between harsh ammonia chemicals and raw, unstandardized mehendi pastes that lacked refinement. RAFA re-envisions this ritual: an exquisite powder formulation enriched with conditioning botanicals that bestows luminous colour while actively nourishing scalp and strands.
