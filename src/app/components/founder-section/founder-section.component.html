Meet Farheen Ravindra

Farheen Ravindra Founder & Formulator Visionary
Nutritionist • Yoga Practitioner • Wellness Formulator

“Farheen Ravindra brings together her background in nutrition, yoga and lifestyle wellness with a special interest in women’s wellness, hair care and skin care. Through RAFA, her focus is to build a thoughtful personal-care brand centred on quality, responsible communication and everyday care.”

Believing that genuine self-care should never be rushed or chemically harsh, Farheen envisioned RAFA as an intimate bridge between classical botanical remedies and contemporary scientific standards. The goal is simple: empowering both men and women to colour and nurture their hair without compromise.
